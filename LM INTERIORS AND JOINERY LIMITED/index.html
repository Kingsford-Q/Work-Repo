The Joiner Behind the Workshop

Luke Mutch, founder.

LM Interiors and Joinery Limited was founded by Luke Mutch to bring proper trade joinery back to fitted interiors — the kind measured on site, cut to the room, and fitted by the person who built it, not handed off down a chain of subcontractors.

The company was incorporated in April 2024 and works across Heywood, Rochdale, Bury, and greater Manchester, taking on a limited number of projects at a time so each one gets a proper site visit before it gets a quote.
