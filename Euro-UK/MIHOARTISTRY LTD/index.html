About MIHOARTISTRY

Built on clarity, culture, and craft.

Our work lives at the crossroads of storytelling and strategy: helping brands, founders, and creative businesses articulate what they stand for and present it with elegance.

MIHOARTISTRY LTD is a newly formed private company incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office details in London and a single-shareholder structure led by Jinghan Lyu. The business reflects a distinct creative direction grounded in professionalism, trust, and rising cultural relevance.

At its core, the company embodies a modern artistic identity: purposeful, premium, and audience-aware. This foundation supports a wide range of client needs, from visual identity and positioning to brand storytelling, creative direction, and market-facing presentation.
