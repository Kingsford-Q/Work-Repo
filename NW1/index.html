We own the buildings we run.

We're an owner-operator, not a fund or an agency. That means the people who agree a purchase or a lease are the same people who look after the building afterwards.

Buy

We acquire freehold and long-leasehold commercial property, from single shops to mixed-use blocks. If a building isn't right for us, we'll say so early.

Let

We let space to businesses on clear terms. We'd rather keep a good tenant for years than chase the last pound of rent.

Manage

Repairs, compliance and service charges are handled directly, by people you can reach, so small problems stay small.
