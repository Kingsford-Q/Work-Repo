The Difference

Why buy through a dealer.

Every piece is sourced, authenticated, and priced by one person — not a marketplace algorithm.

01

Sourced Individually

Each watch and piece of jewellery is selected in person, not bought in bulk from a wholesaler.

02

Authenticated Before Listing

Provenance and condition are verified before anything is offered for sale — not after a query is raised.

03

Viewed by Appointment

Every serious enquiry is met in person where possible, so you handle the piece before deciding.

04

No Pressure to Decide

Pieces are held, not rushed — there is no countdown timer attached to any listing.

Craftsmanship & Care

How a piece reaches you.

Sourcing

Found, not manufactured to order

Pieces are sourced from private collections, estate sales, and trusted trade contacts across the UK — never mass-produced to a catalogue.

Authentication

Verified before it's offered

Provenance, hallmarks, and movement condition are checked against reference records before a piece is ever listed.

Restoration

Serviced where needed

Watches are serviced by a qualified horologist and jewellery is cleaned and inspected for setting security.

Viewing

Handled, not just photographed

Serious enquiries are invited to view the piece by private appointment before any commitment is made.
