From viewing to keys

Four steps, with the same small team from the first viewing to the day you move in.

  1. 1

    View the home

    We show every home ourselves, including evenings and Saturdays by arrangement.

  2. 2

    Apply

    Send ID, proof of income and a previous landlord reference. A holding deposit reserves the home while we check.

  3. 3

    Sign and pay

    We go through the tenancy agreement with you, then take the first month's rent and deposit.

  4. 4

    Move in

    Collect your keys with a photo inventory, safety certificates and your deposit protection details.

Questions tenants ask

Something not covered here? Send us a message and we'll reply within one working day.

Photo ID and proof of your right to rent in the UK, proof of income such as payslips or an employment letter, and a reference from your current or last landlord. If your income doesn't cover the rent, a guarantor can help.

A holding deposit of up to one week's rent reserves the home while we reference you. It goes towards your first payment. The tenancy deposit is up to five weeks' rent. We protect it in a government-approved scheme within 30 days and give you the details in writing.

No admin, referencing, check-in or renewal fees. The only other charges the law allows are things like replacing a lost key or late rent, and we'll list any that could apply before you sign.

Ask us. We look at every request and won't say no without a good reason. Some flats have building rules we need to check first.

Contact us directly and we'll arrange a tradesperson. No heating, no hot water or a leak is treated as urgent.

If you smell gas, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999 first, then let us know.
